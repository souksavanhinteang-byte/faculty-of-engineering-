# Faculty content: evidence and editorial decisions

Accessed on **2026-10-17** (Asia/Bangkok). This is the actual retrieval date, not an article date or a claim that undated pages are current.

The website remains an independent experimental student project. Existing owner-supplied photographs, Facebook URL, Three.js scene, and bilingual interaction are retained. Photographs are not evidence of a named laboratory, research project, partner, or news event.

## Where content lives

- `src/faculty-content.ts`: typed bilingual factual copy, individual department destinations, documented bachelor majors, timeline, resources, dated news, contact details, and the source registry. Each source stores Lao/English URLs, access date, and an internal verification note. Facts reference those registry entries.
- `src/content.ts`: existing bilingual presentation copy and navigation.
- `src/faculty-content.css`: additions using the existing type, color, and spacing system.
- Notes in this document and `verificationNote` fields are editorial metadata, never visitor-interface text. Small localized source links are rendered beside the relevant material.

## Method and access limitations

Started with <https://fe-nuol.edu.la/en/>; the web reader failed, so the supplied fallback <https://fe-nuol.edu.la/en/home-en/> was tried. The web reader also timed out on several other pages. Direct reads with Chrome’s HTTP client succeeded, including the Department body. A later direct request confirmed `/en/` redirects to `/en/home-en/` with HTTP 200.

Read actual page bodies rather than inferring services from menus. English/Lao headings and bodies were compared; missing Lao prose was translated conservatively. No search-result snippets or navigation labels alone support a programme, facility, or service claim. The repeatable read-only helpers are `scripts/read-official.mjs` and `scripts/inspect-contact.mjs`.

The source website is undated in many places and contains placeholders and inconsistencies. Retrieval success does not establish a current programme specification, open opportunity, facility availability, or active partnership.

## History: resolve the count conflict explicitly

The two timeline entries use History in both languages: establishment as Engineering and Architecture in **1995**, then Architecture’s separation in **June 2004** and the remaining faculty’s renaming. These historical dates appear separately from the ten-department discovery figure.

The English History page says **nine departments**, whereas the dedicated Department pages in both languages say **ten**. Use the latter for the department list and do not repeat the historical-page count. The founding-faculty count also differs between English History and other versions; it is omitted. Undated student totals and annual graduate figures differ between pages and are omitted.

## Other exclusions and unresolved source issues

- Chemical, Road and Bridge, and Transportation and Logistics pages do not substantiate detailed curricula. Programme information must come from the faculty directly.
- Mining’s English body says four years of internship; its Lao body says four weeks. Neither duration is reproduced.
- English Computer Engineering text mixes degree terminology; no degree title, study length or credit count is reproduced. Only explicitly listed bachelor majors are shown.
- Facilities lists have inconsistent translations/duplicate entries. Use a few examples present in both versions, without counting the whole inventory.
- Research pages do not establish when examples ran or whether they are active now.
- Student-services content differs by language and is incomplete. Resource links are provided without promising provision or availability.
- Scholarship publication and application years differ; the 2021 application period is historical. No current open call was verified.
- No current calendar, enrolment figure, leadership name, admissions rule, deadline, partnership validity or award claim is published.
- Initial web-reader failures were recovered with direct official-page reads. Remaining limitations concern incomplete/undated content, not an inability to read the pages used for the displayed claims.
